ILLUSTRATIVE CASE STUDIES

Four ways to solve
digital problems.

Each example outlines a hypothetical challenge, a proposed approach and possible deliverables. No client names, project metrics or outcomes are fabricated.

01 · Business website concept

Help a small business explain its offer.

Hypothetical challenge

Visitors need a clearer view of services, trust signals and the next step.

Proposed approach

Organize content around customer questions, build a mobile-first page structure and make contact actions easy to find.

Possible deliverables

Responsive pages, service cards, enquiry form, metadata and launch checklist.

02 · Product launch concept

Make a new digital product easier to understand.

Hypothetical challenge

A new product needs to communicate value without overwhelming visitors.

Proposed approach

Lead with the core benefit, explain features in plain language and add a focused call to action.

Possible deliverables

Launch landing page, feature sections, responsive visual system and enquiry / waitlist flow.

03 · Community platform concept

Help members find useful information.

Hypothetical challenge

Community information is spread across updates, resources and event details.

Proposed approach

Group information into clear sections and prioritize event discovery, resources and contact pathways.

Possible deliverables

Community homepage, event listings, resource hub and member information architecture.

04 · Brand identity concept

Build a more consistent visual presence.

Hypothetical challenge

A growing brand needs repeatable visuals across its website and promotional materials.

Proposed approach

Define typography, color, logo usage and reusable layouts before creating campaign assets.

Possible deliverables

Visual direction, brand starter guide, social templates and website styling tokens.
