QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked
questions.

Clear expectations make projects easier to plan.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on page count, content, design complexity and integrations. Use the estimator as a starting point; WebNexa will confirm a scope-specific quote after discussion.

How long does a website take?

A simple landing page may take less time than a multi-page or interactive website. Timing depends on scope, feedback and whether content is ready.

Can you work with an existing website?

Yes, potential work can include redesigns, responsive improvements, content updates and performance-focused refinements after reviewing the current site.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

Hosting and domain needs are discussed during planning. The current WebNexa showcase itself is designed for static hosting on Cloudflare Pages.

Can I request a custom feature?

Yes. Describe the goal in the enquiry form. Features requiring accounts, persistent data or secure admin tools may need an external service or backend.

Are the projects in the portfolio completed client work?

The large concept catalogue contains illustrative ideas, not claims of completed client projects. Verified case studies should be added only when project details are confirmed.

How do I start?

Send an enquiry with your goal, desired features and timeline. You can also request a meeting through the contact page once a booking provider is configured.
